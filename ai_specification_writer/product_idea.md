Product Idea: Pathway AI
Vision
Make international master's degree research more accessible, transparent, and efficient by helping students identify university programmes that fit their academic background, budget, and personal preferences. Pathway AI will support informed decisions through clear comparisons and links to official university information—not predict or guarantee admission.
Primary Users and Their Needs
- Prospective international master's students: Need to discover relevant programmes without spending hours searching individual university websites.
- Budget-conscious applicants: Need to filter programmes by annual tuition, programme duration, and expected study-related costs.
- Students with nontraditional or uncertain academic profiles: Need to understand published entry requirements, identify potential gaps, and see which details require confirmation.
- Students preparing applications: Need to organize shortlists, deadlines, required documents, and research notes in one place.
Key Features
1. Personalized programme discovery: Filter by study field, country, tuition ceiling, duration, teaching language, and other preferences.
2. Requirements comparison: Display published eligibility criteria alongside a student's profile, clearly distinguishing matches, mismatches, and unknown information.
3. Side-by-side university comparison: Compare shortlisted programmes using consistent fields and direct links to source pages.
4. AI-assisted explanations: Summarize complex admissions information in plain language with links to the underlying official sources.
5. Saved shortlists and application tracker: Let students bookmark programmes, record deadlines, and track document preparation.
6. Data freshness indicators: Show when programme details were last checked and flag information needing re-verification.
Key Differentiators
- Constraints-first matching: Prioritizes real applicant needs—especially tuition limits, programme length, and teaching language—instead of generic university rankings.
- Transparent recommendations: Explains why each programme matches or does not match a student's stated criteria.
- Evidence over speculation: Links to official university sources and labels missing or uncertain data rather than inventing requirements.
- Practical application workflow: Combines discovery, comparison, and application planning in one product.
- Privacy-aware design: Collects only profile information needed for matching and gives users control over saved data.
Initial MVP Scope
The first version will offer a searchable catalogue of selected European master's programmes, profile-based filters, programme comparison, bookmarks, and source-backed requirement summaries. Automated application submission, admission guarantees, and unsupported probability-of-acceptance scores are outside the initial scope.
Success Criteria
Users should be able to find programmes matching their filters, understand the reasons behind recommendations, and verify important details through official sources before applying.
